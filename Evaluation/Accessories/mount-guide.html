Set up while stationary

LumaPath Research · Phone-forward reference · Physical validation pending

These directions can differ. Spatial cues mean left, ahead or right of the phone. Head pose and walking heading are unavailable. No exact distance is encoded by loudness.

  1. Use a protected stationary trial area, your usual mobility aid and a helper if needed. Use a comfortable existing handheld or chest support; no purchase is required.
  2. Keep the rear camera clear and facing the intended view. Check view coverage. A handheld camera has no assumed floor height.
  3. For spatial trials use stereo headphones at a comfortable volume, with environmental sound audible. Confirm that the Bluetooth output is headphones, not a speaker.
  4. In Live view choose Research, channels and mount. Start capture, check the three setup boxes, then choose Confirm phone-forward alignment. Only the mount mode is saved.
  5. Recheck after a large turn, viewport change, tracking loss, pause or headphone change. If the reference is unavailable, use enabled phone speech/haptics and text. Do not infer a safe path.
  6. Pause or End to stop the phone session. On watch, Pause both is confirmed only after the phone replies. If unreachable, pause the phone directly.

Stop when uncertain

All advanced categories and watch delivery remain Research-only. Missing depth is unknown, not a drop. A single watch attention tap does not identify direction; an already issued system tap cannot be retracted. No physical trial or human acceptance is claimed.
